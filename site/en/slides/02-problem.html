THE PROBLEM Making a deck still means choosing between looks, portability and automation

PowerPoint and Keynote

They look good, but they are binary files: hard to review in git, hard to generate from code or with AI.

Web slide frameworks

Flexible, but you inherit packages, a build chain and CDN links, and the result is a folder, not a file.

Screenshots and PDFs

Easy to share, but frozen. Nobody can fix a typo or update a chart without the original.

 slideck-html: the look of a designed deck, the portability of one file and the automation of plain text.
